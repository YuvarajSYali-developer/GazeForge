## GNM: Generative aNthropometric Model (Google LLC), Apache License 2.0
* Source: https://github.com/google/gnm (commit 846ab76); model weights https://huggingface.co/google/gnm-v3 (`v3_0/gnm_head.npz`).
* Used: the head model (template, identity and expression bases, joints, skinning weights, quads, UVs, vertex groups);
  the 68-point landmark definition (`gnm/shape/data/landmarks/head_sparse_68.txt`); the semantic decoder weights
  (`expression_decoder_model.h5`, `identity_decoder_model.h5`), evaluated by our own NumPy code.
* `gazeforge_gnm/gnm_model.py` and `gazeforge_gnm/semantic.py` are independent re-implementations of GNM's forward pass and
  decoder MLPs (checked against the official implementation, see README).
* The `.blend` files contain geometry derived from the GNM model. The model file itself is not redistributed.
* Licence text: `licenses/GNM_LICENSE_Apache-2.0.txt`.
* Citation: Ploumpis S. et al. (2026) "GNM Head: A Generative aNthropometric Model of the human head", arXiv:2607.23687.

`gazeforge_gnm/kinematics/saccade_model.py`, `blink_model.py` are vendored from the GazeForge V3 research release.
Published constants cited inside (Baloh et al. 1975; Van Opstal & Van Gisbergen 1987; Evinger et al. 1991; Willett et al. 2023).

## Physical constants
Skin subsurface mean free paths: Jensen H.W. et al. (2001) "A practical model for subsurface light transport", SIGGRAPH.
Corneal refractive index 1.376 (Le Grand schematic eye).

No photographs, scans or textures of real people were used; all textures are generated by the code in this repository.
