## Methods taken from the literature

* Listing's law / binocular extension: Mok, Ro, Cadera, Crawford & Vilis (1992), *Vision Res.* 32(11);
  van Rijn & van den Berg (1993), *Vision Res.* 33(5/6); Tweed (1997), *J. Neurophysiol.* 77 —
  cited in README §9 to state what the model does **not** implement.
* Sub-pixel pupil-centre refinement (per-ray half-way edge points + trimmed Kasa circle fit):
  standard techniques; Kasa, I. (1976) *IEEE Trans. Instrum. Meas.* IM-25(1).
